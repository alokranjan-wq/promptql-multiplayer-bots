# Website Redesign: typesafe.ai

Redesign www.typesafe.ai so it looks as good as the product is. The voice and the content of the site are already great. Keep them exactly as they are, and change only the visual design.

## The brief

> Make this website look really beautiful aesthetically: www.typesafe.ai. The voice of this website is amazing. Keep the content same but you are free to redesign. The product is amazing. The visual of the website is the only thing that's behind. Do your very best: to make it look aesthetic, beautiful, sexy. The product is so good that the website deserves to visually look amazing. Making it visually stunning with micro-animations, interactive, and have brilliant visual hierarchy. Figure out a plan, then attack.

## Rules

- Keep every word of the copy. Do not rewrite, shorten, or add product claims.
- Keep the site's structure and pages. You are free to change layout, typography, color, imagery, and motion.
- If you cannot reach www.typesafe.ai, say so instead of guessing what is on it.

## Steps

### 1. Study the current site

- Read every page and capture all the copy verbatim.
- Note the voice, the story the site tells, and the order it tells it in.
- Note what holds the current visuals back.

### 2. Make a plan

- Pick a visual direction that matches the voice: typography, color, layout, imagery, and motion.
- Decide the visual hierarchy for each section: what the eye should land on first, second, and third.
- Plan the micro-animations and interactive moments, and where each one earns its place.
- Post the plan in this chat, then start building. Do not wait for approval.

### 3. Attack

- Build the full redesign as a working website with the original content.
- Make it visually stunning, with micro-animations, interactivity, and brilliant visual hierarchy.
- Make it work well on desktop and mobile, and respect reduced-motion settings.

### 4. Review your own work

- Look at the result at desktop and mobile sizes, as a visitor would.
- Critique it honestly against the brief, fix what falls short, and repeat until it is your best work.

### 5. Hand it over

- Share a live preview that everyone in this chat can open.
- Post before and after screenshots of the key sections.
- Summarize the design choices you made and why, in a few lines.
